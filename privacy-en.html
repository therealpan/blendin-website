2. What data we collect

As part of the conversational interview with Blendy and the composition of tables, we process:

  • Identification data: the name you provide and, depending on the channel you choose, your phone number or chat identifier.
  • Interview content: the text messages or voice notes you exchange with Blendy.
  • Structured profile (7 dimensions): sector, business stage, skills, short-term goals, what you offer, what you are looking for and relational style, extracted automatically from the conversation.
  • Personal card: name, summary tags, a short description (narrative) and a QR code generated for the event.
  • Event data: event code, assigned table and matching score.
  • Minimal technical data: information needed to run the service (e.g. language, session timestamps).

3. How we collect it

Data is collected directly from you when you talk to Blendy through one of the three channels: WhatsApp (text or voice notes), web chat (text) or phone call (voice). No installation and no account creation are required.

4. Purposes and legal basis

We process your data to:

  • Create your profile and compose the event tables, matching people by complementarity. Legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected before the interview.
  • Deliver the service and support the organiser in running the event. Legal basis: performance of the requested service.

Providing the data is optional, but without the interview data we cannot generate your profile or assign you to a table.

5. Profiling, AI and matching

BlendIn uses a proprietary AI orchestration (matching and routing developed in-house) to extract the 7 dimensions from the conversation and compose the tables. This is profiling used solely for pairing people at the event.

The system does not make automated decisions producing legal effects or similarly significantly affecting you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR: table assignment concerns the event experience and can be reviewed and changed by the organiser.

6. Voice processing

If you choose the voice channels (WhatsApp voice notes or phone call), the audio is processed and transcribed solely to understand the content of the conversation. We do not use voice for biometric identification and we do not treat it as a special category of data.

7. Retention and deletion

BlendIn is designed on a GDPR by design basis with automatic hard delete: interview and profile data are deleted automatically within 30 days after the event ends.

You can request early deletion at any time through the chat with Blendy or by email. Any data that has been anonymised and can no longer be linked to you may be retained for statistical purposes only.

8. Who we share data with

Your data may be shared with:

  • The event organiser, who receives the profile and the table composition to run the event.
  • Technical service providers that support us in delivering the service, appointed as data processors under Art. 28 GDPR and bound to process data only on our instructions.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

9. Transfers outside the EU

Where some service providers process data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer takes place on the basis of appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

10. Your rights

At any time you can exercise the rights under Art. 15-22 GDPR:

  • Access to your data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (right to be forgotten)
  • Restriction of and objection to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

You can exercise them directly through the chat with Blendy or by writing to blendin@piirz.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Malta, the Information and Data Protection Commissioner).

11. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to protect data from unauthorised access, loss or disclosure, in line with the state of the art and the nature of the data processed.

12. Minors

BlendIn is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data of minors; if you believe this has happened, contact us for removal.
