Last updated: 27 July 2026

This page explains the use of cookies and similar technologies on the BlendIn website, a service by PIIRZ Digital Limited.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store on the user's device to make them work or to collect information. Similar technologies include the browser's local storage.

Cookies we use

The BlendIn showcase site is designed to be lightweight and privacy-friendly. We do not use profiling, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts for typography, which may involve a request to Google's servers. No marketing cookies are set through this service.

Third-party cookies in the conversation channels

When you interact with Blendy via WhatsApp or other external channels, the respective privacy and cookie policies of those service providers apply (e.g. WhatsApp/Meta).

How to manage cookies

You can delete or block cookies and clear local storage at any time from your browser settings. Disabling technical cookies may affect some features of the site.
